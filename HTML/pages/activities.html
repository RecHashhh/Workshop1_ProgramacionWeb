Activities

University is not only about courses. Yachay Tech is a residential campus, which means almost everything happens here: this is where students train, eat, and meet outside class hours. This page covers the three things that fill the gaps in my weekly schedule — student clubs, sport, and where to find food on campus.

Student clubs

The university runs clubs of every kind: academic, sporting and cultural. Joining one is the fastest way to meet people outside your own degree programme, since the groups mix students from every year and every school.

Clubs I am interested in

These are the three I follow, one from each category:

  1. Espacio Recreativo de Salsa y K-pop – cultural. Dance sessions open to anyone, no previous experience required.
  2. Club de Fútbol Masculino – sporting. Trains on the campus pitches and plays in the internal tournaments.
  3. Asociación de Estudiantes de Matemáticas y Ciencias Computacionales – academic. The closest group to my own degree, and the one where side projects usually start.

The full list of groups is published on the student clubs page of the university (opens in a new tab).

Sport and recreation

Training centre

The campus has its own training centre with free weights and exercise machines. Because everything is within walking distance, going to train costs no travel time at all — which is the only reason I actually keep the habit during exam weeks.

Beyond the weights room, the same area has:

  • Table tennis
  • Futbolito – table football
  • Outdoor pitches for football and other sports

Food on campus

Restaurants

There are several places to eat on campus, offering both fast food and lighter, healthier options. The ones I go to most often are:

  • Antares
  • Colibrí
  • Sol Café

There are also vending machines spread around the campus for snacks and drinks, which cover the gaps between classes when the restaurants are closed or the queue is too long.
