Two workshops, one site

William Garzon · Information Technology · Semester II 2026

The same six-page website, delivered twice. Workshop 1 is the structure written in HTML5 alone. Workshop 2 is that same structure with a stylesheet on top, so the two folders read as a before and after of the very same markup.

Open a workshop

Workshop 1 – only HTML

Six interconnected pages with no CSS and no JavaScript. The semantic elements, the schedule table with its rowspan and colspan, and the contact form with its ten input types.

HTML5

Workshop 2 – HTML + CSS

The same markup with a stylesheet: design tokens, a light and a dark theme, an image viewer and a welcome sequence, all without a single line of JavaScript.
